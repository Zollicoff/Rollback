# Episode 2 Scripts

Full scripts for missions 13–26, in the same format as Episode 1: portraits in parentheses, in-mission lines keyed to story triggers, ***(clue)*** on twist foreshadowing.

**New minor characters:** Hattie Crane, a 1920s bootlegging queen. Dwayne, a data-center admin stuck working New Year's Eve 1999. Radio-only voices: Bomber Lead (1943) and Tower (1962). Mentioned but unseen: Professor Harrow and Colonel Stroud.

**Mae's tally this episode:** 1 (M13), 2 (M16), 3 (M19), 4 (M21), 5 and 6 (M22), 7 (M26).
