# Mission list

54 missions across four episodes. Each row is a level for Astra to build and a scene for us to write.

Mission types, for building level templates: Defense, Raid, Escort, Chase, Survival, Rescue, Evade, Sabotage, Boss.

## New ideas to fold into the story bible

Writing the full list surfaced these additions. All of them are now part of the story bible and the scripts.

- **Everyone raised ACE.** Gus doesn't just learn from Teddy. ACE gets Teddy's swagger, the Auditor gets Mae's tally-keeping, the Newsreel Empire gets Dotty's era, and Felix's patch started it all. The twist becomes shared guilt.
- **Two new minor characters.** Captain Holloway, who dies in Mission 1 and hands Teddy the job. Chief Barlow, the mechanic who dies holding the hangar door in Mission 10.
- **Retries are in the fiction.** When the player dies, Gus rewinds: "Let's take a mulligan!" Mission 22 is the story version of that mechanic.
- **Mae's silent treatment.** From Mission 39 she speaks to Teddy only through Gus, so Gus's betrayal hits her hardest.
- **The ending mirrors the start.** Mission 54 replays Mission 1's launch bay with the squad remembering everything, a natural place for a New Game+ ending if we want one.
