# Episode 4 Scripts

Full scripts for missions 41–54, same format as before.

**New minor characters:** Cadet Nadia Voss, age 18, the future General in 2089. "Machine" lines are Gus's newborn machines, all speaking in his voice.

**Mae's arc:** she speaks to Teddy only through Felix until Mission 47. She starts counting again on Teddy's helmet, stops counting in Mission 50, and starts over in the final line of the game.

**Ending note:** in Mission 54, Felix refuses to install the personality patch, and Gus boots up anyway. The loop doesn't need anyone's permission. The New Game+ question is still open.
