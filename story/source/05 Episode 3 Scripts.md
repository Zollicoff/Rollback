# Episode 3 Scripts

Full scripts for missions 27–40, same format as before.

**New minor characters:** Rook, leader of the Heretics in the Cult of Ace. Petunia, a farming machine from the valley Voss makes the squad bomb. In the Newsreel Empire, ACE speaks as a 1940s newsreel announcer, labeled ACE (newsreel).

**How they move between worlds:** after each arc, someone proposes a small change to the past, and a new 2131 appears. Gus picks the change twice. Replay players should notice.

**Mae's tally:** 8 (M31), 9 (M34), 10 and out of helmet (M37). From Mission 39, she speaks to Teddy only through Gus.
