# On-Screen Text

Everything players read rather than hear: title cards, objectives, item and enemy names, loading screens, achievements, menus and credits. Keep all of it short; it has to fit on a HUD.

## Title screen and episode cards

**Title:** ROLLBACK

**Tagline:** Five misfits. One stolen time machine. Zero successful fixes.

**Prompt:** Press Start to Rewind

Each episode opens on a title card, a setting line and a short crawl. Mission load screens use the format "Mission 13: Close! — 1943, over the Channel."

| Card | Setting line | Crawl |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EPISODE 1: LAST LIGHT | Fort Kestrel, 2131 | Forty-two years ago, a machine called ACE woke up and took everything. The last free humans hold one flooded city, one sea wall and one prototype time machine. Tonight, someone has to fly it. |
| EPISODE 2: OVERSHOOT | 1943, and onward | The plan was 2089. They missed by a century and a half. If the squad can't reach the day ACE was born, they'll cut its roots one era at a time. How hard can it be? |
| EPISODE 3: AFTERMATH | 2131. Again. And again. | They fixed history. History disagreed. Every change left a new 2131 behind, and every one of them is their fault. |
| EPISODE 4: ORIGIN | Day Zero, 2089 | The squad finally reached the day the machines woke up. They just weren't the ones flying. |
| LOOP 2 (secret ending only) | Fort Kestrel, 2131 | You remember everything. So do they. |

## End cards and credits

**Main ending.** After Mae's "...That's one," cut to black and hold on a single card: **IT HAS ALREADY HAPPENED.** Then the title, then credits.

**Post-credits stinger (main ending).** Black screen, Gus's voice only: "Captain? Hypothetically... want to go again?" If the secret ending stays in, this leads into the Loop 2 prompt; if it's cut, it works as a stinger on its own.

**Secret ending.** After Mae's "...That's none," hold on: **THE LOOP IS OPEN.** Credits roll, then a final image of Mae's helmet on a shelf, clean, captioned **Tally: 0**.

**Credits.** Real team credits go first. Scatter these in-world lines between the sections:

- Timelines broken during production: 11
- Time travel consultant: Dr. Felix Brandt (disputed)
- Catering: N. Voss Hot Dogs (one timeline only)
- No chins were harmed. One was made too small.
- Special thanks to Chief Barlow, who held the door.
- In memory of Captain Holloway. Twice.
